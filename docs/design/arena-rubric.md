# Rubric (for the judges only; candidates never saw this)

Score each 1-5.

1. **Simplicity of the path.** Landing to poster in few steps, one primary action per screen, no jargon, decimals, scores or weights on the main path. Details exist one level deeper and are findable.
2. **Copy.** Specific (real numbers, dates, times, quotes from the data), dry and warm, no generic quips, no contradictions between facts, every fact has a plain "how we know". Would make a stranger smile in 30 seconds.
3. **The poster.** Restrained, one idea, legible as a thumbnail, typographically crafted (size-specific tracking and leading, deliberate hierarchy). Good without a photo and with one. Real 1080x1920 PNG export.
4. **Interaction craft.** Feedback on pointer-down, interruptible spring motion, the sheet or reveal can be grabbed and reversed, spatial consistency, reduced motion honoured, keyboard reachable.
5. **Phone-first quality.** 390 px wide with no sideways scroll, comfortable tap targets, intentional at 1280 px, contrast.
6. **Portability.** Vanilla ES modules, CSP-safe (no inline styles or scripts, no innerHTML with data), small surface, data-driven from the result JSON, easy to port into the existing Vite app.
